## Participant information sheet

**Study title.** [Title to be agreed], part of the project "Agentic AI for Fostering Critical Thinking Education".

**Who runs it.** Muhammad Eusoff Amir bin Hazri, final year student, Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, supervised by Dr Ho Tatt Wei. Contact [student email] or [supervisor email].

**What the study is about.** We want to find out whether practising with an AI tutor that asks questions helps students reason better than reading the same material.

**What you will do.**

1. Answer a short reasoning test of 15 questions, in about [20] minutes.
2. Spend one hour on the material. You will be put in one of two groups by chance. One group reads the material. The other group works through it with the AI tutor.
3. Answer a second short test of 15 questions, in about [20] minutes.
4. [Optional. Answer one more short test a week later.]

The whole session takes about [2] hours.

**Do you have to take part?** No. Taking part is your choice. It does not affect your marks in any course. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.

**What we keep.** We keep your test answers, and if you are in the tutor group, your answers in the tutor and how the AI marked them. We do not keep your name. You get a participant code from the lecturer, and only the code is stored with your answers. Please do not type personal details in your answers. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app stores only a scrambled form of your Google id, never your name or email.

**AI services.** Your tutor answers are sent to an AI service so they can be marked. [Name the service once known, for example the UTP Azure service.] They contain no name.

**Risks.** There are no known risks beyond the time it takes. Some questions may feel hard, which is normal.

**Benefits.** You practise reasoning, and you may see which kinds of thinking you are good at. The study helps improve how critical thinking is taught.

**How the results are used.** Results are reported only as group numbers, in a final year report and possibly a research paper. No one can be identified from them.

**Keeping the data.** The data is kept for [period set by the ethics committee] in [where], then deleted.

**Ethics approval.** This study was approved by [committee], reference [number].

**Questions or complaints.** Contact the researchers above, or [ethics committee contact].
