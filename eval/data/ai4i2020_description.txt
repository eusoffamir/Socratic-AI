Facts from the dataset page's variable information, read on 28 November 2026 (close paraphrase, not the page's exact words).

The dataset has 10,000 data points, each one run of a milling machine.
Product ID is a letter L, M or H for the product quality variant (low 50%, medium 30%, high 20%), then a serial number. Type is this letter.
Air temperature [K] is generated by a random walk, normalized to a standard deviation of 2 K around 300 K.
Process temperature [K] is generated by a random walk normalized to a standard deviation of 1 K, added to the air temperature plus 10 K.
Rotational speed [rpm] is calculated from a power of 2860 W, with normally distributed noise on top.
Torque [Nm] is normally distributed around 40 Nm with a standard deviation of 10 Nm, with no negative values.
Tool wear [min]: the H, M and L variants add 5, 3 and 2 minutes of tool wear per process.
Machine failure is 1 if at least one of the five independent failure modes below is true.

Tool wear failure (TWF): the tool is replaced or fails at a randomly selected tool wear time between 200 and 240 minutes (120 times in the dataset; the tool is replaced 69 times and fails 51 times, randomly assigned).
Heat dissipation failure (HDF): heat dissipation causes a process failure if the difference between air and process temperature is below 8.6 K and the rotational speed of the tool is below 1380 rpm (115 data points).
Power failure (PWF): the product of torque and rotational speed (in rad/s) equals the power required for the process. If this power is below 3500 W or above 9000 W, the process fails (95 times).
Overstrain failure (OSF): if the product of tool wear and torque exceeds 11,000 minNm for the L product variant (12,000 for M, 13,000 for H), the process fails due to overstrain (98 data points).
Random failures (RNF): each process has a chance of 0.1% to fail regardless of its process parameters (5 times).
